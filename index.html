01 / About the conference

Where music
meets technology.

Welcome to the 28th International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR 2027), taking place in London, United Kingdom, from 12 to 16 September 2027.

ISMIR brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry professionals from around the world to share advances in music information retrieval — spanning music understanding, audio analysis, machine learning, computational musicology, and more.

We look forward to welcoming the global MIR community to London. Further details about the programme, submissions, registration, and venue will be announced here.
